About Juventus

Juventus Football Club, also known as Juve, is one of Italy’s most successful and historic football teams. Founded in 1897 and based in Turin, the club has achieved major domestic and international success.

This fan website provides users with access to player information, club history, match schedules and key Juventus achievements through a clean and responsive design.
